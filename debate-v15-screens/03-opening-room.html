AO Amina OkaforUniversity of Ibadan
+For

Disclosure is an authorship norm, not a punishment. Universities already ask students to cite sources, collaborators, and reused material because assessors need to know what intellectual work the student performed. Generative AI changes the scale of assistance, but not the principle. A short declaration—what tool was used, for which task, and how the output was checked—gives lecturers better evidence while allowing legitimate use for translation, outlining, and feedback.
